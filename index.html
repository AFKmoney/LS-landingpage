Every LazySiren account comes with LazySiren Cloud — our hosted intelligence, ready the second you sign in. No API key to hunt down, no setup screen. Siren answers, codes, forges apps and operates your OS from the cloud, on your phone, your laptop, anywhere you log in.

How it works

LazySiren Cloud is a subsidized house model served from our own endpoint. Your plan sets your budget — messages and tokens per week — and everything is metered transparently in your account panel.

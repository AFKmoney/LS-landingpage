Self-healing

She fixes her own mistakes. Before you see them.

Every AI makes mistakes. The difference is what happens next. With Siren, you don't have to be the one who catches them.

  • Every app is checked before it reaches you. Siren runs what she wrote, finds the real errors and repairs them first.
  • If an app breaks later, she fixes it on the spot. She reads the error, repairs the code and reloads the app.
  • Her actions are verified. When something she does fails, she sees the exact error and tries again the right way.
